# 01 — PROBLEM STATEMENT

## Status
WORKING DRAFT — discussed decisions are not final-locked.

## Problem in One Sentence
People make project decisions based on assumptions and information that can later change, while AI assistants may remember the decision without checking whether the reason behind it is still valid.

## Why This Problem Matters
A decision can become unsuitable when an important assumption changes. Builders need help noticing relevant changes, understanding which decisions are affected, and evaluating recovery options.

## Target Environment
Developer/builder-first projects, including student projects, hackathon teams, indie builders, and small technical teams.

## Current Pain
- Decisions and the reasons behind them are easy to lose or separate from current project context.
- Assumptions that support decisions can become outdated.
- People may not notice the impact of changed assumptions until the project is affected.
- Recovery requires identifying the affected decision, researching alternatives, and deciding what action to take.

## Non-Goals
The following are out of scope for the current MVP:
- Email/calendar assistant
- Giant life-memory system
- Multiple AI personalities
- Voice assistant
- Mobile app
- Dozens of integrations
- Custom model training
- Huge multi-agent system

## Core Differentiator
“Most AI remembers what you decided. Ours remembers why—and knows when that reason stops being true.”
