# 03 — USERS AND PERSONAS

## Status
WORKING DRAFT — audience direction discussed; not final-locked.

## Primary User
- Who: Developers and builders working on projects.
- Context: Student projects, hackathon teams, indie builders, and small technical teams.
- Goal: Keep project decisions connected to their reasons and detect when important assumptions change.
- Main pain: Decisions can become outdated when the information or assumptions behind them change.
- Technical comfort: Technical/builder-oriented users.

## Secondary Users
No separate secondary-user group has been agreed.

## Primary Persona
### Developer / Builder

### Situation
A person or small team makes project decisions based on constraints, evidence, and assumptions that may change during development.

### Needs
- Record decisions and why they were made.
- Track selected important assumptions.
- Understand which decisions are affected by a relevant change.
- Research alternatives that satisfy the original constraints.
- Approve consequential actions before they are performed.

### Frustrations
- Losing the rationale behind a decision.
- Discovering late that an assumption is no longer true.
- Having to reconstruct project context manually.

### Success
The user is alerted to a relevant change, understands its impact, reviews suitable alternatives, and remains in control of consequential actions.

## User Principles
1. Preserve the reasons and context behind decisions.
2. Explain important system outputs clearly.
3. Ask for approval before actions that require it.
4. Do not assume undocumented capabilities or requirements.
