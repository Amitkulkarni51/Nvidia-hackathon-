# 04 — PRODUCT VISION

## Status
WORKING DRAFT — not final-locked.

## Product Name
Undecided.

## One-Line Description
A developer/builder-first Personal AI that remembers project decisions and why they were made, tracks selected assumptions, detects relevant changes, explains their impact, researches alternatives, and can take approved actions.

## Product Goal
Help builders notice when the reasons behind important project decisions stop being valid, understand the impact, and recover with user-controlled assistance.

## Core Value Proposition
“Most AI remembers what you decided. Ours remembers why—and knows when that reason stops being true.”

## Core User Journey
```text
Record a decision and its rationale
    ↓
Select important assumptions to monitor
    ↓
Detect and verify a relevant change
    ↓
Connect the change to an affected decision
    ↓
Explain impact and research alternatives
    ↓
Request permission when required
    ↓
Perform approved action, verify result, update context
```

## MVP Boundary

### Working MVP Features
1. Decision Memory
2. Decision Health Engine
3. Assumption Watch
4. Impact & Recovery Intelligence
5. Controlled Action
6. Decision Timeline

These remain working-scope decisions, not final-locked.

### Explicitly Out of Scope for Current MVP
- Email/calendar assistant
- Giant life-memory system
- Multiple AI personalities
- Voice assistant
- Mobile app
- Dozens of integrations
- Custom model training
- Huge multi-agent system

Specific measurable success criteria have not yet been agreed.
